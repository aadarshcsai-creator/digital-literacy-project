# 🛡️ Stay Safe Online – Prevention Checklist

## 🔒 General Safety Tips
- Never share your UPI PIN with anyone  
- Do not approve unknown payment requests  
- Always verify identity before trusting any caller  
- Avoid clicking on suspicious links  
- Use strong and unique passwords  
- Enable two-factor authentication  
- Keep your apps updated regularly  
- Install apps only from trusted sources  

---

## 💰 Financial / UPI Safety
- Receiving money does NOT require a PIN  
- Always double-check payment requests  
- Use official banking apps only  
- Do not share OTPs with anyone  

---

## 🚨 What To Do If Scammed
- Immediately inform your bank  
- Call cybercrime helpline: 1930  
- Report on: https://cybercrime.gov.in  
- Block your account if needed  

---

## 🎯 Best Practices
- Stay aware of latest scams  
- Educate friends and family  
- Think before clicking or sharing  

---

## 🧠 Final Note
Cyber safety is everyone’s responsibility. Being cautious and informed can prevent major financial and personal losses.
